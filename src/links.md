# Links

[Dale DeSharone](https://blog.hardcoregaming101.net/2012/09/dale-desharone-unspoken-legend.html) (credited as Dale Disharoon) designed and programmed the game, working with Snyder to on the plot and dialogue to be a continuation of the trilogy, and with [William Groetzinger who created the art](https://www.timeextension.com/features/the-making-of-below-the-root-the-1984-metroidvania-masterpiece-that-predates-metroid-and-castlevania).

Many other people have kept Green-Sky alive through memories, writing, and guides.
Here are the original materials, interviews with the creators, other players' recollections, reviews, and playthroughs.

## Original materials and reference

- [The original manual](https://archive.org/details/below-the-root-game-manual-1984) (Internet Archive). Instructions, characters and Snyder’s account of designing the game.
- [Museum of Computer Adventure Game History](https://www.mocagh.org/loadpage.php?getgame=belowtheroot). Box, map, manual and disk photographs. [Manual PDF.](https://www.mocagh.org/spinnaker/belowtheroot-manual.pdf)
- [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Below_the_Root_(video_game)). Historical overview and bibliography.
- [MobyGames](https://www.mobygames.com/game/602/below-the-root/). Platform information, credits and screenshots.
- [Lemon64](https://www.lemon64.com/game/below-the-root). The C64 game entry and players’ memories.
- [The Cutting Room Floor](https://tcrf.net/Below_the_Root). The hidden editor and other discoveries inside the game.

## The game and its makers

- [John Szczepaniak: The making of Below the Root](https://www.timeextension.com/features/the-making-of-below-the-root-the-1984-metroidvania-masterpiece-that-predates-metroid-and-castlevania) (Time Extension, 2024). Bill Groetzinger recalls creating Green-sky, alongside accounts from Dale DeSharone and Zilpha Keatley Snyder.
- [Dale DeSharone — an unspoken legend](https://blog.hardcoregaming101.net/2012/09/dale-desharone-unspoken-legend.html) (Hardcore Gaming 101, 2012). The earlier interview cited by Time Extension.
- [Zack Smith: Weird Children’s Books: Zilpha Keatley Snyder and Below the Root](https://zacksmithwriter.wordpress.com/2013/04/18/weird-childrens-books-zilpha-keatley-snyder-and-below-the-root/) (2013). How the game continues the novels and changes their ending. Spoilers for both.
- [Compute!, September 1985](https://www.atarimagazines.com/compute/index/index.php?issue=issue64). Issue index listing Nick Piazza Jr.’s contemporary review.

## Reviews and recollections

- [Justin Stahlman: Below the Root: a story, a computer game and my lifelong obsession](https://blog.stahlmandesign.com/below-the-root-a-story-a-computer-game-and-my-lifelong-obsession/) (2015). Childhood discoveries, a proposed remake, a complete 10240×2560 map and extracted sprites.
- [Phil Salvador: Below the Root](https://obscuritory.com/platform/below-the-root/) (The Obscuritory, 2020). On hospitality, trust and the game’s kindness.
- [Mark Krepela: Below the Root](https://www.lemon64.com/review/below-the-root/377) (Lemon64, 2004). Exploring Green-sky, its atmosphere and its frustrations.
- [Wingnut: Below The Root — Commodore 64 — 1984](https://bestretrogames.blogspot.com/2012/03/below-root-commodore-64-1984.html) (2012). Discovering the C64 game years later, with a map and gameplay video.
- [Darby McDevitt: Digital Future, Invisible Past](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/digital-future-invisible-past-what-lives-on-when-a-good-game-dies-) (2014). Returning to Below the Root and the difficulty of keeping old games playable.

## Playthroughs and guides

- [Data Driven Gamer: Game 373: Below the Root](https://datadrivengamer.blogspot.com/2023/07/game-373-below-root.html) (2023). A C64 playthrough with maps and detailed observations. [Completion and assessment.](https://datadrivengamer.blogspot.com/2023/07/below-root-won.html)
- [The Adventurers’ Guild: Below the Root](https://advgamer.blogspot.com/2011/11/game-1-below-root-1984.html) (2011). A newcomer follows the clues in the PC version. [The final part.](https://advgamer.blogspot.com/2011/12/game-1-below-root-won.html)
- [TarynB93: Guide and walkthrough](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/c64/581563-below-the-root/faqs/57342) (GameFAQs). Quest solutions, maps and an account of discovering the hidden level editor.
