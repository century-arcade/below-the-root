In Green-sky, people make their homes high in the trees. Glide between branches to visit
them, and learn to sense the feelings behind a stranger’s welcome. A conversation can lead
you further into the forest; a new power can help you grow a branch across a gap you
couldn’t cross before. Exploring means getting to know this strange world and the people
who live here.

Below the Root is a game Saul loved as a child. We’re restoring the 1984 Commodore 64 game
for browsers so it can be played again and shared with others, carrying its world, art,
words and music forward.

Whether you’re returning after decades or discovering Green-sky for the first time, you’re
welcome here. Play free in your browser, or watch the demo for a first look around.

<div class="about-details">
<section aria-labelledby="the-game">
<h2 id="the-game">The game</h2>
<figure class="box-back"><a href="/assets/box/back.jpg"><img src="/assets/box/back.jpg" width="454" height="604" loading="lazy" alt="Back of the cream, navy-bordered box, with the original description and screenshots of a shop and treetop platforms."></a><figcaption>The original box back</figcaption></figure>

The game’s world comes from Zilpha Keatley Snyder’s trilogy of novels: *Below the Root*,
*And All Between* and *Until the Celebration*. Snyder helped design the game as a
continuation of the books, shaping its world and writing dialogue with designer and
programmer Dale DeSharone, credited as Disharoon. William Groetzinger created the art, and
Windham Classics published the game in 1984.

You can begin without having read the books. A troubling dream sets a quest in motion, and
the people you meet hold clues to what threatens Green-sky. Learning whom to trust
matters: someone may offer food or a place to rest, while another may put you in danger.
Following those clues and developing your powers gradually gives you new ways through the
forest.

</section>
<section aria-labelledby="the-port">
<h2 id="the-port">Restoring a way back</h2>

We’ve studied how the original game works and rebuilt it for the browser, using its own
pictures, text and music. Our care goes into preserving that work and making it easier to
reach today. You can start here without setting up an emulator or swapping disks.

If the controls are new or half-remembered, the Help button explains them. Use the arrow
keys or WASD, or play with a mouse, touch or gamepad. The built-in map helps you find your
way, revealing more outdoor areas as you explore. Visible status keeps food, rest and the
passing days in view, while the forest still asks you to watch your step and plan your
journey.

When it’s time for a break, autosave keeps your progress in this browser on this device so
you can continue later.

For how the restoration is built and checked, see the [project README and
source](https://github.com/century-arcade/below-the-root) and [the
specification](https://github.com/century-arcade/below-the-root/tree/master/docs/spec).
Problems can be reported in the [issue
tracker](https://github.com/century-arcade/below-the-root/issues).

</section>
