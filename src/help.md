
## Keyboard

- **WASD/Arrows**: Move
- **Space/Enter**: Select/skip/jump
- **F**: Open menu / select a choice
- **Up/Down**: Previous/next choice; release between moves
- **M**: Map

## Recording playback

Seek back/forward by room:

- **Left/Right**: 1 room
- **Shift+Left/Right**: 10 rooms

## Developer mode

- **Delete**: Go back a day
