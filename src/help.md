Move through Green-Sky, talk with its people, and discover what lies below the root.

## Keyboard

- **Move:** arrow keys or WASD.
- **Button:** Space, Shift, or Ctrl to skip, jump, or select.
- **Pause:** Escape or P. Move or press the button to resume.
- **Map:** Tab or the Map button during a quest.
- **Help:** ? or H, or the ? button. Escape closes Help.
- **Options:** O or the Options button for volume, CRT effect, classic display,
  and debug tools.
- **Fullscreen:** F, when supported.
- **Mute:** M.
- **Volume:** - and = (or _ and +).
- **Skip a tune:** press the button while the game waits for its music.
  Available in the modern display.

## Touch

Hold to push toward your finger. Tap to press the button that way.

Tap a door or double-tap a spot to walk there. While walking, tap to re-aim
or tap the figure to stop.

## Gamepad

Use the D-pad or left stick to move. Any face button skips, jumps, or selects.
