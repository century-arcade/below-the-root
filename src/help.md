
- **WASD/Arrows**: Move
- **Space/Enter**: Select/skip/jump
- **F**: Open menu / Choose
- **M/Tab**: Map
- **Escape**: Close menu/map/help, otherwise pause
- **P**: Pause/resume
- **?/H**: Help

## Recording playback

Seek back/forward by room:

- **Left/Right**: 1 room
- **Shift+Left/Right**: 10 rooms

Focus the game for keyboard seeking; hold an arrow to repeat. The room
buttons also seek one or ten rooms. Backward seeks skip brief visits.
Space/Enter skips a waited tune in the default display.

**Play from here** takes over the recording and replaces your autosave with
its latest boundary. **Play**, then **CONTINUE**, returns to your original quest.

## Developer mode

- **Backspace/Delete**: Rewind to the previous room entry.
- **R**: GitHub issue reporting/login on a configured hosted site.

Rewind discards later moves. Autosaves and downloads retain the latest
quest-start, room-entry or completion boundary, not unfinished mid-room play.
