
## Keyboard

- **WASD or Arrows**: Move
- **Space**: Trigger (select/skip/jump/menu)
- **P**: Pause (Move to resume)
- **Tab** Map
- **?**: Help
- **O**: Options
- **F**: Fullscreen
- **M**: Mute/Unmute
- **+/-**: Adjust Volume

## Touch/Mouse

Press and hold or double-tap on a location to move your character there.

Tap on your character to open the menu.

Tap on a door to go through it.
