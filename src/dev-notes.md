# Dev notes

[This is a slightly-abridged transcript of what it took to put this project together.  With all of the experiments and misdirections and back-and-forths that I explored as I figured out what it was I wanted to say.  I offer this as both "proof of human" and also in my perpetual hope that someday, someone will find it useful.]

Human time: 117 hours (110 sittings)
LLM tokens: 1.2 billion (44m uncached, 5m output)

Thanks to the makers of the original game: Zilpha Keatley Snyder (story), Dale Disharoon (program), William Groetzinger (artwork), Jim Graham, Leonard Lebow and Vince Mills (programming), and Graphic Ink (handbook design), published by Windham Classics in 1984.

Sources: disk images and box scans from the preservation release by Count Zero/SCS*TRC/CyberpunX; [manual scans](https://archive.org/details/below-the-root-game-manual-1984) from the Internet Archive; [inside-lid photograph](https://www.mocagh.org/loadpage.php?getgame=belowtheroot-alt) from Howard Feldman's Museum of Computer Adventure Game History; [Commodore 1702 photograph](https://c-64.nl/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/1702_monitor_commodore_64.jpg) from c-64.nl; power-button sound from [iiiDIY's 1702 restoration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SQE4ViGvLes).

Fonts: [Cinzel](https://github.com/NDISCOVER/Cinzel), [Jost](https://github.com/indestructible-type), [Michroma](https://github.com/googlefonts/Michroma-font) and [Open Gorton](https://github.com/dakotafelder/open-gorton).  Tools: the [VICE](https://vice-emu.sourceforge.io/) emulator, [marked](https://marked.js.org/) and [Playwright](https://playwright.dev/).

Further reading, reviews and playthroughs are on the [Resources](/resources) page.
