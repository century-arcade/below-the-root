power-on.mp3, power-off.mp3: the Commodore 1702 monitor's push-button power switch.

Both synthesized for this port; no recordings.

Click: a short noise burst through two resonators (300 Hz body knock,
2.2 kHz plastic tick), about 25 ms.

Degauss (power-on only), 15 ms after the click: synthesized, modelled on
real degauss recordings.

Same click for on and off: a push-push switch sounds alike both ways.
